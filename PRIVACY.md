# Koma privacy policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Koma is a home-screen launcher. It has no account, no analytics, no ads and no server of its own. Everything you set up stays on your phone.

## What stays on your device
Your layout, settings, notes, tasks, Focus history and imported fonts are stored in the app's private storage. Nothing is uploaded, and backups are turned off.

## Permissions and why they are used
- **Contacts:** show starred contacts and find a contact when you search. Names and numbers are read on the device and never sent anywhere.
- **Call log:** show your recent calls on the Recent calls tile.
- **Calendar:** show upcoming events on the Calendar tile.
- **Approximate location:** pick the place for the Weather tile. Only rounded coordinates are sent (see below).
- **Internet and network state:** fetch weather and open web searches in your browser.
- **Usage access (optional, you grant it in system settings):** show screen time.
- **Notification access (optional):** let the Music tile read and control the playing media session. Notification content is not read or stored.
- **Accessibility service (optional, Focus Lock):** detect when a blocked app opens during a Focus session so Koma can send you home. It does not read screen content, record input or send anything off the device.
- **Vibrate, Wi-Fi state:** timer feedback and the Device tile.

You can refuse or revoke any permission. The matching tile then simply stays empty.

## Data sent off the device
The Weather tile sends your approximate coordinates to a public weather service (Open-Meteo) to get a forecast. No identifier or account is attached. Web searches open in your own browser with the search engine you chose, and that engine's policy applies from there.

## Children
Koma is not directed at children and collects nothing from anyone.
